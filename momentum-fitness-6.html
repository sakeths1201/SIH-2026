Why not another fitness app?

What big apps get wrong

Studies on MyFitnessPal, Strava & Fitbit show users quit from shame, paywalls, lost streaks, and Western-only design. Momentum is built against that.

🚫 Anti-shame, not calorie police

Big apps trigger guilt logging “bad” food. We never shame meals. Fuel is optional. Effort counts on hard days.

💚 Streaks with grace days

Miss a day? You don’t start at zero. Grace days + a Comeback boost protect motivation instead of punishing life.

📚 Student & hostel reality

Exam mode, 5–10 min study-break moves, no-equipment hostel workouts, rainy-day plans. Campus life—not a Silicon Valley gym.

🇮🇳 Indian meals as fuel (not guilt)

One-tap log for roti, dal, idli, dosa, rice, curd—without Western barcodes or sugar warnings on a banana.

🔒 Offline + your data stays yours

Works with zero internet. No forced account. Export anytime. Nothing sold to advertisers.

⚡ Energy budget, not just steps

Tell the app how drained you are. It suggests shorter or gentler moves so you still show up without burnout.
